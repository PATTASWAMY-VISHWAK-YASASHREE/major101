## Key Transitions

```
2013-2016 → Radiomics + traditional ML + first CNNs
         ↓
2017-2019 → 3D CNNs dominate; transfer learning matures; U-Net/V-Net
         ↓
2020-2021 → Transformer revolution; ViT, MAE, nnU-Net (peak year)
         ↓
2022-2023 → Explainability, Uncertainty, Survival become mainstream
         ↓
2024-2026 → Multimodal (MRI+CT) fusion; translation models; experimental
```
